# StraftatEights
Repo for creating experiences in Straftat akin to Call of Duty "Eights" and more.

# Goals
- Support for up to 10 players, or maybe even more.
- Adding multiple game modes and the ability to rotate through them automatically.
  - CTF
  - Hardpoint
  - Search and Destroy
  - Gun Game
  - Juggernaut / HVT
  - Michael Meyers
  - Border Patrol
- Ability to limit and control movement
  - Things like HVT or CTF flag carrier cannot walljump or slide
  - Make the game feel more grounded (controversial, optional, configurable)

# Why Straftat?
I know a lot of people will read this and think "just go play Cod" or "just play Straftat as it is".
Straftat is the perfect canvas for something like this. Round mechanics, hundreds of maps, tons of guns, excellent multiplayer, all under 13GB.

Adapting it to fill the void in FPS party games is a no brainer to me and already tons of people are asking for more game modes and even making them.
